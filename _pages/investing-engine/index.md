**A private research and personal-finance application**

Investing Engine is a personal software project developed for learning, experimentation, investment research, and financial data analysis.

It forms part of my broader interests in data science, artificial intelligence, financial markets, and software engineering, alongside the projects showcased on my [personal portfolio](https://apsinghanalytics.github.io/).

### Purpose

Investing Engine helps me explore ways to automate and improve personal investment research, financial recordkeeping, and data-driven decision-making.

The application may integrate with third-party services, including the Gmail API, to retrieve and organize relevant financial emails, investment notifications, and document attachments.

### Availability

Investing Engine is an independently developed hobby project intended for my own personal use. It is not currently offered as a public application, commercial service, or investment advisory product.

There are no plans to offer general public access in the foreseeable future.
