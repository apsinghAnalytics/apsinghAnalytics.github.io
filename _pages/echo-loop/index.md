**A private podcast player for my own Google Drive audio files**

Echo Loop is a personal Android app that plays audio files from a Google Drive folder I choose. It is a hobby and learning project, built for my own use.

It forms part of my broader interests in software engineering and learning tools, alongside the projects showcased on my [personal portfolio](https://apsinghanalytics.github.io/).

### Purpose

Echo Loop helps me listen to my own audio library, such as lectures, talks, and podcasts that I keep in Drive. It remembers where I stopped, queues up what to play next, and lets me bookmark and revisit moments I want to remember, including hands-free in the car.

The app connects to Google Drive to list and play the audio files in the folder I choose.

### Availability

Echo Loop is an independently developed hobby project intended for my own personal use. It is not published on the Google Play Store, and it is not offered as a public application or commercial service.

There are no plans to offer general public access in the foreseeable future.
